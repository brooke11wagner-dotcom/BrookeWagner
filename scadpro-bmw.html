SCAD Pro Industry Collaboration

BMW Brazil

A SCAD Pro collaboration with BMW Brazil focused on reimagining the automotive finance experience through AR technology

My Role

I led the AR Simulator section of this project — developing the concept, building the ideation, and prototyping the experience in Unity. The AR feature allows customers to visualize a BMW in their real environment before committing to a finance decision, bridging the gap between aspiration and purchase.

About the Project

Making Financing Feel Like a Win

This was a SCAD Pro collaboration with BMW Brazil, tackling one of the brand's real challenges: many Brazilian consumers have never financed a vehicle and feel anxious about the process. Our team was challenged to design a solution that combined clarity, personalization, and trust to make the financing journey feel as exciting as the car itself.

My Contribution

The AR Simulator

The AR Simulator lets customers place a life-size BMW in their real environment using their phone — no app download required. The concept was born from the insight that seeing yourself in the car makes the finance decision emotional, not just rational. I developed the concept, contributed to ideation, and built the prototype in Unity.

The core challenge: combine AR visualization, clear support, and luxury customization in one seamless experience

Point, deploy, and see your BMW appear in real space

Built in 10 weeks — iOS (ARKit), Android (ARCore), no app download required (WebAR)

BMW Dream Garage — the AR experience extended to real-world activations at Carnival and Interlagos
